## Understanding today's communication problems through theory

Artificial intelligence is changing how people create, share, and interpret messages. This project introduces key schools of communication theory and uses their ideas to examine the challenges AI brings to everyday communication.

The site is designed for curious readers, not specialists. Each of the five theory pages will offer a different lens on a shared communication problem. Together, they can help us ask not only what new technologies can do, but how they shape meaning, relationships, and public life.

### Explore the lenses

- [Rhetorical tradition]({{ '/traditions/rhetorical/' | relative_url }}) — persuasion, audiences, and public discourse.
- [Semiotic tradition]({{ '/traditions/semiotic/' | relative_url }}) — signs, symbols, and the making of meaning.
- [Phenomenological tradition]({{ '/traditions/phenomenological/' | relative_url }}) — lived experience and understanding between people.
- [Cybernetic tradition]({{ '/traditions/cybernetic/' | relative_url }}) — information, feedback, and communication systems.
- [Critical tradition]({{ '/traditions/critical/' | relative_url }}) — power, ideology, and whose interests communication serves.

These pages are a framework for work in progress. The specific case study and analysis will be developed here.
