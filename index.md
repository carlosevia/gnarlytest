## Understanding today's communication problems through theory

Artificial intelligence is changing how people create, share, and interpret messages. This project introduces key schools of communication theory and uses their ideas to examine the challenges AI brings to everyday communication.

The site is designed for curious readers, not specialists. The five pages below provide different lenses for exploring communication in the age of AI. The problem to analyze is intentionally left open for the author to define.

### Explore the lenses

- [Transmission]({{ '/traditions/transmission/' | relative_url }}) — communication as the sending and receiving of messages.
- [Semiotics]({{ '/traditions/semiotics/' | relative_url }}) — signs, symbols, and the creation of meaning.
- [Interpretation]({{ '/traditions/interpretation/' | relative_url }}) — how people make sense of messages.
- [Encoding-decoding]({{ '/traditions/encoding-decoding/' | relative_url }}) — how messages are produced and interpreted.
- [Algorithmic intentions]({{ '/traditions/algorithmic-intentions/' | relative_url }}) — the purposes and assumptions behind algorithmic communication.

These pages are a framework for work in progress. The author will add the problem and analysis.
